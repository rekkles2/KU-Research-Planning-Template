## Scope of Work - 4 Questions

In this section, the essence of the proposed work is described by answering four key questions.

### What is the problem you want to address in your work?

The objective here is to have students give a very brief and to the point description (1 sentence) of the problem they want to address within their paper.

### Why is this problem important?

Explain why the identified problem matters by addressing the following questions:

- What are the consequences of leaving this problem unresolved, and how do they affect the task?
- What problems have previous studies addressed?
- Why are existing approaches unable to resolve the identified problem?

### What solution does your work propose?

Explain how your method addresses the identified problem and qualitatively describe the outcomes it is expected to achieve.

### Why does the proposed solution work?

Identify the key component of your method and explain why it is effective. Design a compelling and rigorous experiment to evaluate the following:

- How previous methods perform on the identified problem.
- How your method performs on the same problem.
