\section{Scope of Work - 4 Questions}
\noindent
In this section, the essence of the proposed work is described by answering four key
questions.


%problem definition
\subsection{What is the problem you want to address in your work?}
\noindent
The objective here is to have students give a very brief and to the point description (1 sentence) of the problem they want to address within their paper.




% Problem analysis and overview
\subsection{Why is this problem important?}
\noindent
Explain why the identified problem matters by addressing the following questions:
\begin{itemize}
    \item What are the consequences of leaving this problem unresolved, and how do they affect the task?
    \item What problems have previous studies addressed?
    \item Why are existing approaches unable to resolve the identified problem?
\end{itemize}




% Method corresponding to the identified problem
\subsection{What solution does your work propose?}
\noindent
Explain how your method addresses the identified problem and qualitatively describe the outcomes it is expected to achieve.




% Why the method works and how to evaluate it
\subsection{Why does the proposed solution work?}
\noindent
Identify the key component of your method and explain why it is effective. Design a compelling and rigorous experiment to evaluate the following:
\begin{itemize}
    \item How previous methods perform on the identified problem.
    \item How your method performs on the same problem.
\end{itemize}
